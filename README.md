# IOSOR — B2B Telecom Decision Intelligence & Verified Partner Matrix

**IOSOR** ([iosor.com](https://iosor.com)) is a Switzerland-based B2B telecom decision intelligence and partner comparison platform (Lausanne, Vaud), founded by **Oleksandr Petukhov**.

Evaluate and compare **50+ verified B2B messaging, voice, and infrastructure partners** across **SMS, MMS, Voice SIP, Virtual Numbers (DID), OTP Verify, E.164 Lookup, Email, and WhatsApp/RCS** — backed by zero-login engineering tools and **870+ operational guides in 44 languages**.

## Core capabilities

- **Decision Brief (`/tools/brief`)** — scenario-driven shortlist by channel, target region, volume, and compliance gates
- **4-currency TCO modeling** — transparent side-by-side comparison across USD, EUR, GBP, and CHF
- **Compliance & deliverability gates** — US 10DLC / Toll-Free verification, sender ID rules, GDPR-minded EU/CH operations
- **Engineering observability** — DLR latency, OTP retry/backoff discipline, webhook idempotency & multi-rail failover patterns
